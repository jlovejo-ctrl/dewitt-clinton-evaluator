SUPER SIMPLE SETUP - NO FILE EDITING NEEDED!
=============================================

Good news! This version is MUCH easier - you don't need to edit any files!

STEP 1: Unzip the folder
- Download dewitt-clinton-evaluator-FINAL.zip
- Double-click to unzip it
- You'll see a folder with your files

STEP 2: Open the app
- Double-click "index.html"
- It opens in your browser

STEP 3: Enter your info
You'll see TWO boxes:
1. Access Password: clinton2025 (or whatever you changed it to)
2. Claude API Key: Paste your API key here (the sk-ant-api03-xxx thing)

STEP 4: Click "Access Tool"
That's it! You're in!

STEP 5: Use the app
- Upload lesson plan + transcript
- Answer the questions
- Generate evaluations

=============================================
TO USE IT ANYWHERE (NOT JUST YOUR COMPUTER)

Right now it only works on your computer. To make it work from anywhere:

4. Share the URL with your team

Want help with that? Just ask!

=============================================
TO CHANGE THE PASSWORD
=============================================

If you want a different password than "clinton2025":

1. Right-click index.html
2. Open with TextEdit (Mac) or Notepad (Windows)  
3. Find: if (password !== 'clinton2025')
4. Change 'clinton2025' to your password
5. Save

That's it! Much easier! 🎉
